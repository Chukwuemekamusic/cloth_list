The hard question: why build another one?

Before deciding whether to build, you need a clear answer to at least one of these:

1. Niche — are you targeting a specific geography (e.g. Nigeria, West Africa) or category (e.g. African prints,
   vintage Ankara, designer streetwear) where existing apps perform poorly?
2. Platform gap — is there a resale platform popular with your users that these apps don't support?
3. UX gap — are these tools too complex or expensive for the reseller community you know?
4. Distribution — do you have direct access to a community of resellers who'd use it?

Want me to /grill-me you on this so we can find the real angle, or do you want to sketch out a target user first?
